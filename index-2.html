Stage design
Tonight's stage was built from things that already existed.

Theatre sets are often built for just a few nights, then thrown away, adding tonnes of wood, fabric and paint to landfills. For this performance, the goal was to create as little waste as possible. Before anything was made, materials were sourced in two ways: by reusing what already existed in storage from past productions, and by taking materials that were already headed for landfill.
